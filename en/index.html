FAQ

What clients want to know first.

Is this worth it for a small business?

Especially then. In small teams, the most important people often handle the routine on the side. Just 5 hours a week at €50 per hour adds up to around €11,500 a year. Use the calculator above to check your own case.

Why now and not later?

Every month the process runs by hand costs time and money you won't get back. Getting started is small: a €690 audit, after which you know exactly what's worth doing.

Wouldn't off-the-shelf software be enough?

Sometimes, yes. Then we'll tell you in the audit and recommend the right tool. We only build custom where ready-made solutions don't fit your systems, rules or data protection requirements.

Where do the automations run and where is our data?

On your own server, on your premises or in a German data centre. For AI steps, we agree together which model may see which data, and put it in writing.

What running costs are there?

Your server costs, AI usage by consumption if applicable, and, if you want it, Automation Care from €149 per month. Every item is in the quote upfront.

What happens if a workflow fails?

Every workflow has built-in error handling: a failure triggers an alert immediately, the case is logged and can be re-run once fixed. With Automation Care, we take care of it.

What if the AI gets something wrong?

Unclear cases are flagged and go to a person. Bookings, payments and customer replies require approval from your team before anything happens.

What if you're unavailable one day?

Everything runs on your infrastructure, uses widely adopted standard tools and is documented. You have all the access, and any experienced developer can take over. You're not dependent on us.

Which tools do you use?

n8n as the automation platform, custom code where building blocks aren't enough (e.g. custom APIs, OAuth2, signed webhooks), and AI models of your choice, local or EU-hosted.

Do we need technical knowledge?

No. You know your processes, and that's enough. We handle the technology, setup and training.

How much time could your team win back?
